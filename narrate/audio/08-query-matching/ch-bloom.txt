This one surprised me. Every component type hashes to five bits of a sixty four bit word. An archetype's mask is the bitwise or of all its types' masks. A query's mask is the or of everything it requires. Then the first test is one and operation: if the required bits are not all present, this archetype cannot possibly match, and it is rejected without reading a single type number. The source even explains the choice of five bits: fewer and different types collide, more and or-ing enough masks together hides a missing type. It is a probabilistic reject in front of an exact one, exactly like a cache tag in front of a tag comparison.
