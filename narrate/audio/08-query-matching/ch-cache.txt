A match records two things: the archetype in the query's list, and a back pointer to the query on the archetype. Then all the matching archetypes' chunk lists get flattened into one array of chunk indices, with a parallel array saying which archetype each chunk came from. Archetypes holding zero entities are skipped during that flatten. And the whole thing is guarded by a valid flag, which somebody else drops for you whenever a structural change touches a matching archetype.
