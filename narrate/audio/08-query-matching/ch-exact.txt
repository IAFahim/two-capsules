Survivors go through the cheap categorical rejects first. Disabled, prefab, system instance, meta chunk. All four are excluded unless you asked for them, which is why your first query mysteriously misses your prefabs. Only after that does it walk the two sorted arrays, the archetype's types and the query's required types, and check the required set, the excluded set, the any set. That walk is linear and it is the expensive part, which is precisely why the bloom filter sits in front of it.
