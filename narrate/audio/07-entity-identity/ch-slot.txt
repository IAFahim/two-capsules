The index selects a slot, and the slot is itself one packed word holding the version, the chunk, and the position inside that chunk. Then comes the detail I enjoyed most. A slot whose version has its low bit clear holds no live entity. Live means odd. That is the same trick as an even and odd sequence counter in lock free code, and it means the null entity, whose version is zero, falls out of the rule for free rather than needing a special case.
