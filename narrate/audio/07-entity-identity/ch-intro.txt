An entity is not an object and it is not a pointer. It is one sixty four bit word, and if you look at the source you find something better than the two integers everyone quotes. It is a single raw field with three bit fields packed into it: twenty eight bits of index, twelve bits of type identifier, and twenty four bits of version. Index and version are properties that mask and shift, not fields you can take the address of. Which means comparing two entities is one compare of one word, and nothing else.
