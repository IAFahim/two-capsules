First, the hash code of an entity is its index alone, with the version thrown away, so a recycled handle collides with the one it replaced in a hash map. Second, sorting entities sorts on index, again ignoring the version. Third, the position inside a chunk is stored in a single byte, which is exactly why a chunk can never hold more than a hundred and twenty eight entities however small your components are. The identity design and the chunk capacity limit are the same decision seen from two ends.
