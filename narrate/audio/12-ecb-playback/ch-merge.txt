At playback, every non-empty chain becomes one entry in a min heap keyed on the sort key of its next command. Pop the smallest, run that chain until it would pass the next smallest key, push it back with its new head key, repeat. That is a k-way merge of already-sorted sequences, exactly the algorithm you would use to merge sorted files. Which means the order commands are applied in depends only on the keys, and not at all on which thread recorded them or in what order the threads ran.
