The package ships two default variants for physics and they are the entire contract. Velocity is replicated, linear and angular, quantized to a thousandth of a unit, and only sent to clients that are predicting the ghost. Graphical smoothing is restricted to server and predicted client versions, which is a deletion rather than an addition, so that an interpolated client does not have physics smoothing fighting the snapshot interpolation already positioning the body. That table is complete, and the completeness is the point. Mass, gravity factor, damping and colliders appear nowhere in the netcode runtime. They are not replicated, and therefore they are never rolled back. Mutate one inside the prediction loop and the mutation applies once per replayed tick and is never undone.
