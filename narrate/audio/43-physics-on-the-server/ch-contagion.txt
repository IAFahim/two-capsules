A predicted body and an interpolated body are in different time domains. One is ahead of the server, one is behind it. They are never simultaneously correct, so a contact between them is a contact between two different moments, and it resolves differently on every machine. In a game where everything is physical that rule propagates. If the player can push a crate, the crate is predicted. If the crate can knock a barrel, the barrel is predicted. The predicted set is the transitive closure of what can be touched by something predicted. Now the arithmetic. The server steps once per tick, sixty times a second by default. The client steps once per tick it is replaying, and the replay depth is roughly round trip time converted to ticks. On an ordinary connection that is around eight solves per rendered frame where the server does one. The server's cost scales with body count. The client's scales with body count times replay depth.
