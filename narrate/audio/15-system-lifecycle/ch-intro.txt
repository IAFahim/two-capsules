There is a temptation to imagine a scheduler here, something that decides which systems are eligible this frame and builds a list. There is nothing of the kind. The group walks its sorted array, and for every entry it evaluates one condition: the enabled bit, ANDed with a requirement test. True runs your update. False does not. That is the entire gating mechanism, and it is written the same way for both the managed and the unmanaged flavour of system.
