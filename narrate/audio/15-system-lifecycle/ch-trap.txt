Here is the sharp edge. The requirement test checks each required query with an emptiness test that deliberately ignores filters. That means enableable components do not count, and shared component filters do not count. Require a component that exists on a thousand entities and is disabled on all thousand of them, and the gate opens anyway. Your update runs, your query matches nothing, and you spend an afternoon convinced the gate is broken. It is not. It was answering a coarser question than you asked.
