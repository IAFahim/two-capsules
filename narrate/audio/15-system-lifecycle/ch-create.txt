Your create callback runs once, when the world is built, before any group has ticked. It runs whether or not the system will ever update again. This is where queries are built and where you declare what your update requires, and both of those are structural decisions that then hold for the life of the world. If you are used to lazy initialisation on first use, unlearn it here: by the time the first frame happens, every system in the world has already had its create callback run.
