Turning a system off does not remove it from anything. It stays in its group's sorted array, it is visited every frame, the sort is not recomputed, and turning it back on costs nothing but the next evaluation of that if. This is worth internalising because it sets the price of the feature. Toggling systems is a branch not taken, so it is cheap enough to do per frame and per world. Adding or removing a system at runtime is not that; that is a structural change to the tree and a resort.
