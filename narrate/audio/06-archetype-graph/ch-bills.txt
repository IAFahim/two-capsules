Then two things happen that nobody warns you about. The new archetype recomputes its own chunk capacity from the sum of the strides, so a chunk that held a hundred and twenty eight entities might now hold ninety, and the ceiling is a hundred and twenty eight no matter how small your components are. And every archetype keeps a list of back pointers to the queries that match it, so touching its chunk list drops the cached chunk list of every one of those queries. That second bill is not paid here. It is paid by whichever system iterates next.
