Think about how many edges you would have to store. An archetype with twenty component types has twenty ways to remove one, and thousands of plausible ways to add one, and every one of those would need a slot. So they do not cache the edges. They recompute the key every time, because the key is one hash over a few hundred bytes and one probe. It is cheaper to redo the lookup than to remember the answer. That trade shows up all over this codebase once you start looking for it.
