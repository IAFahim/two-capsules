Here is the part I did not expect. There is no graph. Nothing stores an edge saying from this archetype, adding a transform gets you to that one. What happens instead is that the runtime copies the sorted array onto the stack, inserts one element in its sorted position, hashes the whole thing, and probes an open addressed table. Hit means an archetype with that exact set already exists. Miss means one gets created on the spot. The set is the key, and that is the entire data structure.
