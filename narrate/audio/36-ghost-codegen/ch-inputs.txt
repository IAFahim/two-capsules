Three attributes carry the declaration. One goes on fields and carries quantization, smoothing, composite change tracking, a sub type selector, and a send flag. One goes on the type and carries prefab type, send optimisation, owner send type, and whether children replicate. The third goes on the type and simply says: replicate the enabled flag as well as the fields. Alongside them sit the templates, one per supported field type, in quantized and unquantized variants.
