Serialization does not delta against the previous value. It deltas against a prediction of the current value, derived from three baselines using fractions in sixteenths, falling back to the newest baseline whenever the extrapolation would be worse. The residual is then written through an entropy model, so a near zero delta costs a handful of bits. A body moving at constant velocity is almost free on the wire. It is the same structure as a lossless audio codec.
