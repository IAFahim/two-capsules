There are no transition edges in the graph at all. A node simply names a state. The machine resolves the name, builds the path that name implies, and then walks both paths together until they diverge. Everything above that divergence is shared, so it is never exited and never re-entered. Which means a jump between two siblings deep in the tree leaves every parent untouched, and a jump to your own current state is a no operation for free. The arithmetic is the mechanism.
