The thin client world is not a client with rendering turned off. It is built from a different filter entirely, so the systems that were never marked for thin client simulation do not exist in it at all. No presentation, no rendering, no interpolated ghost application. It holds a connection open and generates command traffic, which is exactly what you want for testing fifty players. The surprise arrives when something you assumed was universal turns out to have been marked client only, and simply is not there. That includes, in this project, the subscene loading that brings up the client graph.
