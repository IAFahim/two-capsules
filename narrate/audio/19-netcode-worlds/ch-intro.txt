Press play in the editor and you get two worlds. A server world and a client world, in one operating system process, sharing one address space. It is tempting to read that as one program wearing two hats. It is not. They have separate entity managers, separate singletons, separate copies of every system, and they talk to each other over a socket. Nothing crosses between them except bytes on a connection. If you keep that one fact, most of the confusing behaviour in this package stops being confusing.
