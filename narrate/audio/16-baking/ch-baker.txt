Each baker gets exactly one authoring component and is expected to write components onto an entity. It cannot query the scene. It cannot see what the baker next to it did. That looks like a limitation until you notice what it buys: because a baker can only reach what it explicitly asks for, and every ask is recorded, the system knows precisely which bakers to re-run when you drag a slider. Change one field and one baker runs. The restriction is the entire incremental story.
