Every read a baker makes is registered as a dependency. That record is the cache key. And the failure mode that follows is the single most common baking bug there is: read something without declaring it, and the value is baked correctly the first time, then never updated again. You edit the asset, nothing happens, you assume the runtime is broken, and you spend an hour in the wrong file. The bake was never re-run because as far as the record is concerned nothing you touched was an input.
