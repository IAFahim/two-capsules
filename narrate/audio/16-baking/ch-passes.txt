Bakers are not the whole compile. First a pre-baking group runs, for cleanup before anything is read. Then every baker. Then transform baking, which resolves hierarchies into world matrices. Then the ordinary baking systems, which unlike bakers can query the entire scene at once and are therefore where anything cross cutting belongs. Then linked entity groups, then companion objects, then a post-baking group, and finally the strip. Eight ordered phases, all inside a world that is created and thrown away in one call.
