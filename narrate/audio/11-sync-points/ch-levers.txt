First, record the change into a command buffer instead, so many small barriers collapse into one barrier at a known system where you can see it. Second, use switchable components, so turning a thing off flips a bit in a mask rather than moving an entity between chunks. Third, operate on a whole query at once rather than one entity at a time, because the barrier is per operation, not per entity. And if you genuinely need a barrier every single update, there is an attribute for that, which is at least honest about what it is doing.
