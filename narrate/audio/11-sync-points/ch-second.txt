After the change lands, two more things run. Any newly created archetypes are offered to every existing query so they can be matched. Then every archetype whose chunk list changed walks its back pointers and clears the valid flag on each matching query's cached chunk list. Nothing is rebuilt right then. The rebuild happens the next time somebody iterates, which means the cost of your structural change is billed to a system that had nothing to do with it. That is the part that makes profiles confusing.
