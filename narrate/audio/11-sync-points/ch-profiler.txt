The wait is wrapped in a profiler marker named, helpfully, Complete All Jobs. If you have never gone looking for it, go and look. And notice the early exit: if no fences are registered at all, the whole thing is skipped. So a frame where you scheduled nothing pays nothing for its structural changes, and a frame with a deep job graph pays for the longest chain in it. The cost of a barrier is not a property of the barrier. It is a property of what was running when you hit it.
