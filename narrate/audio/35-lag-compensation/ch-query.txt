The read has four behaviours. A tick newer than anything stored returns the live world. A tick in range returns its clone. A tick older than the ring is clamped to the oldest slot. And an uninitialised history returns the live world. The short overload of that call throws away the expected tick and the returned tick, so a high latency client is served the oldest world you have, forever, with no signal at all.
