The history is thirty two collision world slots plus thirty two parallel tick fields. The slot is the tick modulo the size, which is why the size must be zero, one, or a power of two: the index is a mask, not a division, and it has to stay correct when the tick counter wraps. The tick stored beside the slot is the tag. A read hands you back the tag it found, so you can check that the line you got is the line you asked for.
