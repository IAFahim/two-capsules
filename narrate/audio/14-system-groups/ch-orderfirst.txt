Inside simulation there are three buckets, not one. A group can pin itself to the front, pin itself to the back, or say nothing and land in the middle. Four groups on this map pin themselves to the front, and then order among themselves with before and after edges. Network receive comes first because packets have to be in memory before anything reads them. Ghost simulation comes next because snapshots have to be applied before prediction restores from them. Then prediction. Then the ordinary fixed step. Read those four attributes in order and the frame stops being mysterious.
