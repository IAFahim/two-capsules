Every box on this map is at the position an attribute put it, and every one of those attributes was read out of the packages rather than remembered. That matters more than it sounds, because most of what people believe about this tree is folklore. The one fact worth carrying away before anything else: prediction runs before the ordinary fixed step, not after it. If your mental model has physics first and rollback second, it is backwards, and the attribute that proves it is four lines from the top of the netcode prediction file.
