Here is the thing nobody tells you. Netcode does not reuse the engine's fixed step group for prediction. It declares a second one, pinned to the front of the prediction group, and that is the one that replays. The outer fixed step is still there, still running, and it runs after the whole prediction loop has finished. So if you put a physics step in the outer group expecting it to be rolled back, it will not be. It will run once per frame, downstream of a replay that never saw it. The two groups have almost the same name and completely different lives.
