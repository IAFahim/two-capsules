There is more in the real tree. Command buffer systems bracket every group, pinned first and last. There is a variable rate group and a late simulation group. Command send and command receive sit inside ghost simulation and network receive. BovineLabs adds several of its own around scene loading and transforms. None of them are drawn here, and that is deliberate, because the mechanism for finding any of them is identical: open the file, read four attributes, and you know exactly where it runs.
