Initialization, simulation and presentation are created in every world before any of your systems exist. They are not registered, they are hardcoded as the three things that count as roots. Each is spliced into a different phase of the engine's frame. And they are not symmetrical. The presentation group carries an attribute saying its children default to presentation only, which is why an unfiltered system placed in it never reaches a server. The simulation group carries no attribute at all, which is why an unfiltered system placed in it reaches every game world including the server.
