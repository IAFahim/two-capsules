Write a system, add no attributes at all, and it goes into the middle bucket of the simulation group, in every game world. Not into initialization. Not into prediction. Not nowhere. It runs once per frame, after every group that pinned itself first, on client and server and local worlds simultaneously. Half of all why did that run twice questions end right there. The other half end at the trap in the ordering attributes: before and after only work between systems in the same group. Point one across a group boundary and the engine logs a warning and ignores it.
