Here is the thing the dropdown never tells you. A client does not have one clock. It has two, running at different offsets from the server, and every replicated entity is assigned to one of them. One domain plays back the server's past out of a buffer. The other guesses at the client's future and gets corrected. They are not two settings on a spectrum. They are two different machines, and this map puts them side by side so you can see that.
