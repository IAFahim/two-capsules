Take the interpolated side first, because it is the one you already know from hardware. Snapshots arrive and go into a ring. A read pointer trails the write pointer by a fixed number of ticks. You read out whatever the buffer held that many ticks ago, and you interpolate between the two entries that bracket it. The client never simulates this entity. Not once. It is a delay line with a lerp on the output, and like every elastic buffer it can be late, but it cannot be wrong.
