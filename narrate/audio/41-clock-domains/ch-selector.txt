So which domain does an entity get? For the two simple modes it is fixed at bake time and it is the same everywhere. Owner predicted is the interesting one. It selects per machine, at runtime, by ownership. The client that owns the entity predicts it. Every other client interpolates it. Which means the very same prefab, spawned once on the server, is sitting in different time domains on different machines at the same instant. That is not a bug in your mental model. That is the design.
