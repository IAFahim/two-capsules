And now the edge in the middle of the map, the one drawn in red. A predicted entity cannot correctly interact with an interpolated one. They are not simulating the same instant. One of them is roughly half a round trip ahead and the other is roughly two snapshots behind, so a collision between them is a collision between two different moments. The consequence is that anything a predicted entity touches must itself become predicted. Prediction is contagious. You set out to predict one capsule and you discover you have signed up to predict the crate it pushes, the platform it stands on, and the door it opens.
