The asymmetry is brutal and worth stating in numbers. An interpolated ghost costs two buffer reads and a lerp. No gameplay system runs for it on the client at all. A predicted ghost costs ticks behind, times entities, times your predicted systems, every single frame. And the ticks behind term is the same for all of them, because there is one loop covering the whole predicted set. So the only lever you really have is the size of that set. Owner predicted exists to make that set exactly one.
