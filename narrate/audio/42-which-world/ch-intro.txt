The phrase world zero is real, and it does not mean what it sounds like. It names the default collider partition inside one entity world, the one a body belongs to unless you say otherwise. An entity world is an address space. A physics world index is a bucket of bodies inside one of those address spaces. One entity world can hold several physics indices, and every index exists separately on the client and on the server. Saying physics runs in world zero is like saying a process runs in heap arena zero. True of the allocator, silent about the process.
