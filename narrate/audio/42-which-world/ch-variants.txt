So how does the client's visual attach to the server's fireball? It does not. There is no attachment. They are two entities in two address spaces and nothing points from one to the other. What crosses the wire is an integer: a ghost identifier and a prefab index. The client looks that index up in its own prefab collection and instantiates its own local copy. Both worlds baked the same authored asset, so both already agree what index seven means. And the two copies are not identical, because baking emits one prefab plus a set of per world removal lists. At startup each world picks the list for itself and strips its own copy before the first instance exists. Renderers and effects are removed on the server. Authority logic is removed on the client. Same asset, different entities.
