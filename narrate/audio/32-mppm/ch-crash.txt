Two server worlds means two attempts to bind the same port. The first succeeds, the second fails, and then the failure cascades. Subscene loaders find two server worlds where they expected one. Entity assertions fire. The console fills with errors that have nothing to do with sockets, and the port bind message is buried three screens up. The cascade is louder than the cause, which is exactly why this one is hard, and it is a good general lesson: the loudest error is rarely the first one.
