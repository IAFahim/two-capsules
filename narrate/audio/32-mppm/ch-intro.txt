Multiplayer play mode gives you extra editor instances so you can test two clients without building. The temptation is to read them as a lightweight simulation of a second player. They are not. Each one is a genuinely separate editor process, with its own worlds, its own sockets, its own domain, and no shared memory of any kind. Two facts about them do all the damage, and both are about sharing: they share your library folder, and they share your editor preferences.
