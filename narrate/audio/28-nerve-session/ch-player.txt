A player is a controller and a controlled entity. The controller carries the account identity and is the durable half; the controlled entity is the body. When a connection drops, the controller is not destroyed, it is disabled, and it waits. When someone reconnects with the same account, the spawner finds that controller, re-points ownership and the command target at it, and switches it back on. Persistence without a save file, because the identity is a string rather than a network number. It is also why two players in our test show up as four ghosts, and why three would mean two clients had adopted the same controller.
