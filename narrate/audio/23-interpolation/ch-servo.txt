The read pointer does not jump to where it should be. It corrects a tenth of the remaining error per frame, with its rate clamped between eighty five hundredths and one and a tenth of normal speed. Only an error above ten ticks makes it give up and snap outright. So the read pointer is a phase locked loop with a bounded slew rate and a fallback to a hard resync. Every visible smoothness property of interpolated motion comes from those three numbers, and all three are on the same settings struct.
