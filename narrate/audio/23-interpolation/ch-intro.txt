The decision about which entities are interpolated at all belongs elsewhere. This map assumes that decision is made and opens the box. Inside is a queue with two pointers moving at different rhythms, a depth that is not the depth you configured, a clock that slews rather than steps, and an extrapolation path that most people are told does not exist. If you have ever tuned an elastic buffer between two clock domains, you will recognise every single part of this, including which knob you will reach for first and why it will be the wrong one.
