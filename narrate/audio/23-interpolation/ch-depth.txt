Here is the first surprise. You configure a window of two network ticks. That is a floor, not a value. Alongside it netcode measures the actual gap between arriving snapshots, keeps a mean and an approximate deviation, and computes a competing depth as mean plus one and a quarter deviations. The larger of the two wins, clamped at six network intervals. So a jittery link deepens your buffer on its own, without telling you, and the latency you measured on a clean connection is not the latency your players get.
