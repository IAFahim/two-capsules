To produce a value, the lookup walks the ring from newest backwards. Anything newer than the target becomes the after waypoint, overwritten each time it finds something closer. The first slot at or older than the target becomes the before waypoint. Then the factor is the distance from before to target divided by the distance from before to after, plus the fractional part of the tick if we are mid tick. Two known good points and a position between them. Late, but never invented.
