And here is where the usual teaching is wrong, including this book's own interpolation chapter. If there is no slot newer than the target, and the ghost is dynamic rather than static, the lookup does not hold the last value. It takes the before waypoint and the one behind it, runs that pair forward, and inverts the factor. That is extrapolation, and it is on by default. It is clamped at twenty simulation ticks, after which it holds. A static ghost never takes that path, and a ghost with exactly one slot has its factor pinned to zero and does not move at all.
