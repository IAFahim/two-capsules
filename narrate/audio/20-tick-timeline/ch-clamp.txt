One last mechanism, and it is the sort of detail that only exists because somebody hit it. Push forced latency high enough and the predicted tick falls behind the interpolation tick. The four timelines would be out of order. Netcode refuses. It drags the interpolation target forward to meet the predicted tick, which quietly lengthens the interpolation window. So the two knobs are coupled at the extreme. Tune one far enough and you have moved the other, and nothing in the inspector tells you that happened.
