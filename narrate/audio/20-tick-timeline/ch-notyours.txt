And here is the sting. The effective latency is not simply the value you set. It is the larger of your setting and whatever the ping forces. If round trip plus slack exceeds the maximum predict ahead time, five hundred milliseconds by default, netcode raises the latency itself to keep the prediction window bounded. A high ping player therefore gets forced input latency whether or not anyone configured it. In a single world host there is a third contributor: an off frame adds one more tick. So the fourth timeline can appear on somebody else's machine and never on yours.
