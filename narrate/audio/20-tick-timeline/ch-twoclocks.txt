From the estimate, subtract the interpolation depth and you have the tick you render other players at. From the same estimate, add round trip time in ticks plus a command slack margin, and you have the tick you are predicting. Each of those has its own rate multiplier, nudged by a fraction of the current error every frame, and each multiplier is clamped: prediction between nine tenths and one and a tenth, interpolation between eighty five hundredths and one and a tenth. They are servo loops. They slew, they do not step, unless the error gets large enough to give up and snap.
