Now the fourth. The tick you stamp your input with is not the tick you are predicting. It is the predicted tick plus the effective input latency. By default that latency is zero, the two numbers are identical, and the fourth timeline sits exactly on top of the third. That is why everyone counts three. Set forced input latency above zero and they separate: the client keeps predicting the near tick while your keypress is filed for a later one. Your capsule stops answering instantly, and in exchange the rollback depth drops sharply. That is the trade, stated as an offset.
