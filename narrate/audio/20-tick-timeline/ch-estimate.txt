Here is the part that should feel familiar from hardware. The client has no wire carrying the server's tick counter. What it has is an estimate of the tick it expects to receive next, maintained as an exponential average of how wrong it has been. Every frame the estimate is advanced by elapsed ticks and then corrected against what actually arrived. Both of the client's clocks are derived from that estimate, in opposite directions. So this is not clock recovery from an edge. It is a phase estimator with a feedback term, and everything downstream inherits its error.
