This is the part that costs people days. The default filter flag has two expansions and which one you get depends on who is asking. When a world is being populated, default expands to local simulation plus presentation, and that is a literal line of code. When a system is being classified, default expands to whatever its enclosing group declares as its child default, walking up the ordering chain. If that walk reaches a group with no parent, the fallback is local plus server plus client. That second one is what you meet in practice, because the root simulation group carries no filter attribute at all. Think of the flags as a decoder's address map. Default is not an address. It is an instruction that says: decode from the enclosing region.
