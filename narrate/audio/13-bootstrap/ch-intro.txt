Here is the first strange thing about this engine. You never write the line that creates your world. Before the first scene has loaded, before a single script has woken up, the engine reflects over every type you have compiled, looking for one that says I will take over world creation. If it finds one, it hands the whole job over. If it does not, it builds one plain world and gets on with it. Either way, the thing your entities live in was constructed by a function you did not call, from a list you did not write.
