The two halves stamp their spawn ticks from different clocks. Inside the prediction loop the client stamps the tick it is simulating. Outside it, the client can only stamp the last full server tick it knows about. The server stamps its own tick at the end of its frame. The package's own comment on that branch says the two will normally differ by about one tick, and by more when a rate of fire is involved. That is why classification is a window and not an equality test.
