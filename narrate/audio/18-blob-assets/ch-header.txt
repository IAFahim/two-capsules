Immediately before the data sits a fixed thirty two byte header carrying a validation pointer, the length, the allocator handle and a hash. The validation pointer is the interesting one: it is set to point just past itself, and checking it is how a use after dispose becomes an assert instead of a page of garbage. When a blob is serialized that field is written as null and filled in on load, which tells you something about the intent. The header is a runtime concern. The data is the artifact.
