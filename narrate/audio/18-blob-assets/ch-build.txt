You do not build a blob in place. The builder hands out allocations from scratch chunks as you ask for them, wherever they happen to fit, and remembers what points at what. Only at the end does it compute the total size, allocate one contiguous region, copy everything in, and rewrite every internal pointer as an offset. That two phase shape is why you construct a root first and then allocate arrays inside it, and why you can build a tree without knowing its size up front.
