Here is what the relative pointers actually buy. When a section file loads, the loader walks the components that hold blob handles, and for each one replaces a stored byte offset with the batch base address plus that offset. One word written, per reference, per entity. It never goes inside the block. It never touches an array, never follows a chain, never walks a graph. So the fixup cost is proportional to how many things point at your blob, and completely independent of how big the blob is. A hundred megabyte navigation mesh costs the same to load as an eight byte one.
