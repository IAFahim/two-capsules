A million entities can share one block with no duplication and no synchronisation, because the block is immutable by contract and every job can read it at once. The engine defends that contract with an attribute saying certain types may only live in blob storage, and a compiler analyser that refuses to let you copy one out by value. That defence is not pedantry. Copy a blob array field into a local and you have copied the offset without its context, so it now points at whatever happens to be that far from your stack frame.
