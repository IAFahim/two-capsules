The single most common confusion is that a node is one thing. It is four. In the editor it is a box you can drag, and that is the source code. After the importer runs it is a small settings object, and that is the object file. After baking it is a run of bytes inside a blob, and that is the image. And at runtime it is a static method, which is the instruction set. Four artefacts, four assemblies, one idea. Every mystery about grove dissolves once you can say which of the four you are looking at.
