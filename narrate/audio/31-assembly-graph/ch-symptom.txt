Here is why it costs hours rather than minutes. The assembly that did not compile produces no error. The error you get is a type not found, in your own file, on your own line. Which looks exactly like a missing import. So you check your imports, then the spelling, then the namespace, then you start doubting the package version. The one thing you do not check is whether a completely different assembly, that you did not edit, silently ceased to exist. The probe is one shell command: list the compiled assemblies folder and look for the name.
