First, where the inputs come from. A version define says: if this package is installed, define this symbol. Ours declare seven of them, one per package we care about. The scope is the thing to notice. A version define applies to the assembly that declares it, and nowhere else. Symbols do not inherit through references. That is why the same block of seven entries is copy pasted into every assembly definition in this project and in every one in the BovineLabs stack. It is not laziness. There is no other way to do it.
