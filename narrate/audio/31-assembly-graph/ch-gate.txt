Then the constraint list. Seven symbols in ours: two for the graph packages, one for netcode, three for the Unity services, one for the platform toolkit. Every one of them must be defined or the assembly is skipped. There is no OR. There is no partial compilation. We hit this by stripping the package manifest to a minimum and discovering that an entire BovineLabs assembly containing sessions, ownership and approval had quietly stopped existing.
