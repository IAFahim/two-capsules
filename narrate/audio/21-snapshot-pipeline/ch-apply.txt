The update system is where the split happens. For an interpolated ghost it reads at the interpolation tick, finds the two slots that bracket it, and blends. For a predicted ghost it reads at the tick being predicted, pins the blend factor to zero, and uses the result as a rollback point rather than a display value. One buffer, one arrival path, two entirely different meanings depending on a flag on the entity. That is the cleanest place in the whole package to see that prediction and interpolation are two readers of one queue.
