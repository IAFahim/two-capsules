Writing continues until the bit budget runs out, and then it stops mid list. Nothing is dropped, nothing is queued, nothing errors. The chunks below the line simply were not sent this tick, and their age term keeps climbing, so they rise in the sort and go out on a later one. Starvation is prevented by ageing rather than by a queue. This is bus arbitration with a priority that increments while you wait, and if you have built one of those in hardware you already know its failure modes.
