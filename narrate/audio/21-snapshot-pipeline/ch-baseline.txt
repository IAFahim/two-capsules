Now the encoding, and this is the part that surprises people who expect a broadcast. The server does not encode one snapshot and send it to everybody. It keeps a per connection history of what it sent and which slots each client acknowledged, and it delta encodes against that client's own acknowledged state. Two clients on the same tick receive genuinely different bytes for the same entity, because they last confirmed different ticks. This is why send cost scales with connections times ghosts and not with ghosts alone.
