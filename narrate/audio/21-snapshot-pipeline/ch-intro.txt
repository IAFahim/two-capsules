A snapshot is not a picture of the world. It is as much of the world as fits in one packet, chosen in priority order, encoded as differences against what this one client already acknowledged. Every word there is doing work. As much as fits: there is a hard byte ceiling. Chosen in priority order: the list is sorted before a single bit is written. This one client: the encoding is different for every connection. And differences: almost nothing on the wire is an absolute value.
