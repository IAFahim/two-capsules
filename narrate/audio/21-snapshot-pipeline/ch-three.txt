It goes further than a simple difference. For each entity the serializer finds up to three acknowledged baselines and uses them to extrapolate a prediction of the current value, then encodes only the residual against that prediction. Constant velocity therefore costs almost nothing: the extrapolation is right and the residual is near zero, and a near zero residual costs a handful of bits after entropy coding. That is a linear predictor plus an entropy coder, which is a familiar shape if you have looked at any lossless codec.
