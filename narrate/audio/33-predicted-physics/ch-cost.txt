Rollback cost is predicted entities multiplied by replayed ticks, and physics multiplies both terms. A solve is broadphase, narrowphase, and a constraint pass over every body in the predicted world, repeated for each replayed tick. Batching replayed ticks is the emergency valve, and it is a real accuracy loss, because a batched step pushes a larger delta through the solver and contacts resolve differently. Shrink the predicted set before you reach for batching.
