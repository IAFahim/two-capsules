This is the part that surprises people. A section is not deserialized into your world. It is deserialized into a separate world flagged as streaming, one per section in flight, four in flight by default. That world is populated only with systems carrying a process after load filter, so almost nothing you wrote exists there. Your world never sees a half constructed section, and a system running in your world genuinely cannot observe the intermediate state, because the intermediate state is in a different address space.
