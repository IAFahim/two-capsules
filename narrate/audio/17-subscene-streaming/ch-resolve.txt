Before anything loads, a resolve step reads the section header and creates one meta entity per section, hanging them off the scene entity in a buffer. At that moment the world knows how many sections exist and where they are, and holds none of their contents. The request then propagates from the scene entity down to the section entities unless you asked it not to. So the granularity you are really controlling is not scenes; it is sections, and there is an entity per section sitting there waiting to be switched on individually.
