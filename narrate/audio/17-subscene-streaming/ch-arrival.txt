When the streaming world is finished, the entire section is moved across in one bulk operation, and by default only one world is moved per update. Two consequences follow and both are visible in a profile. The section appears all at once, in a single frame, rather than trickling in over several. And the frame it arrives on is the expensive one, because that is where the chunks are relocated and the references are remapped. If your loading hitch has a sharp single frame spike rather than a broad hump, this is where to look.
