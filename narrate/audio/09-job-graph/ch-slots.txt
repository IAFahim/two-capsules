The read slots are a fixed size array. One write fence, seventeen read fences, per type, for every type the type manager knows. When the eighteenth reader arrives there is nowhere to put it, so the seventeen are combined into a single handle, that handle goes back into slot zero, and the count drops to one. It is a spill, and it is the moment the graph quietly gets coarser. Those seventeen jobs are now indistinguishable from each other to anything that comes later.
