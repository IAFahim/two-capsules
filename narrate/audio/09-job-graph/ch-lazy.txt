Before your update runs, the system just sets a flag saying it will need the fences. It does not fetch them. The first time you actually touch the dependency, the fetch happens and the flag clears. If you never touch it, nothing is combined at all. On the way out, if you scheduled anything, the runtime submits the batched jobs and writes your returned handle into the fence slots of every type you declared. That is the step that makes the next system's dependency correct without either system knowing the other exists.
