Three things get dropped before the graph is ever built. A zero sized type that is not switchable is removed from both lists entirely, because a tag has no data to race on. Declaring a write on a type you also read removes it from the reading list, so write wins. And a job that declares no types at all is registered as a reader of the entity type itself, purely so that the next full barrier still catches it. That last one is not about correctness of ordering, it is about not leaking a job that nobody will ever wait on.
