Everything on this map is one allocation. Sixteen kilobytes, taken from a pool, reused. Sixty four bytes of header at the front, deliberately a whole cache line, and then nothing but arrays until the end. There is no object graph here, no vtable, no pointer to follow. If you have ever drawn a memory map on a whiteboard to explain a driver, this is that drawing, and it is the honest layout rather than a metaphor for one.
