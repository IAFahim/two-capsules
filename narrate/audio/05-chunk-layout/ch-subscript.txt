Here is the part that takes a while to sit right. Entity number three does not exist anywhere as a contiguous object. Its transform is in one array, its ghost identity is in another, its input is in a third, and those three pieces may be thousands of bytes apart. What ties them together is nothing but the shared index. That is structure of arrays, chosen for exactly the reason you would choose it in C, and it is why a system that only wants transforms never pulls a single byte of anything else into cache.
