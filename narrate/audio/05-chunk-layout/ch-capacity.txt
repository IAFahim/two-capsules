Capacity is the usable buffer divided by the sum of the strides. So adding one component to an entity does two things at once. It changes the archetype, which means the entity has to move to a different chunk, which is a memory copy of every one of its components. And it changes capacity, so a block that held one hundred and twenty eight entities might now hold one hundred and thirteen. That is what a structural change actually costs, and that is why you batch them.
