Here is a sentence worth memorising, because it is where people get burned. Remote calls arrive in the order they were sent, relative to each other. Nothing whatsoever is guaranteed about a remote call relative to a snapshot. Send one announcing a state change and then send the snapshot carrying that change and you must assume either order. That is why go in game is a remote call and the ghost stream is not, and why a remote call announcing something the snapshot also carries is a race you will lose intermittently.
