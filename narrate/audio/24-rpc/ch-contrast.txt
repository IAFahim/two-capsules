Now the contrast that gives this map its name. Both channels are the same connection and the same socket. They differ only in which pipeline stages the transport composed. The reliable one acknowledges, retransmits, and delivers in order. The unreliable one carries snapshots and commands, keeps the newest, and never makes up a loss. Snapshots are unreliable on purpose: a lost one is not worth resending, because a newer one with better truth is already twenty milliseconds behind it. Retransmission would deliver stale data late.
