Sending is a structural change. You add your component and a send request to a fresh entity. The send request holds one field that matters: which connection to send it to, or nothing at all, which means broadcast. That is why sending a hundred of them in one frame is a hundred entity creations, and why doing it in a hot loop shows up in the profiler as a structural change storm rather than as network cost. The wire is not usually your problem here. The entity manager is.
