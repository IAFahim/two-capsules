A timeline is the cheapest thing you will ever replicate. The authored asset is baked into the build, so it is identical on every machine, and everything visible about a playing timeline follows from two numbers: which timeline, and how far into it. That is a short identifier and a start tick. Six bytes, written once when the action begins and never changed again. Compare that to streaming a character's transform, which costs bytes on every single tick. Replicating the cause instead of the result is the whole argument, and it is a good one.
