On a dedicated server, the rate manager re-enters the whole simulation system group once per pending tick, pushing a tick sized delta each time. The timeline is evaluated once per tick, at integer tick boundaries. On the client, the rate manager lets the group run exactly once per rendered frame, and the delta it pushes is how far the predicted target tick moved that frame, fractional part included. So the two sides advance the same sum with differently shaped steps, and land up to a tick of phase apart. Nothing ever brings them back together.
