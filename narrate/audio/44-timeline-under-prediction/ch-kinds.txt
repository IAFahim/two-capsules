Clips sort into exactly four kinds. Value clips are sampled and pure, and run predicted in both worlds. Cosmetic clips run on the client only, gated so they fire on the first full prediction of a tick and not on every replay. Spawn clips route through a predicted spawn request and get matched against the authoritative spawn instead of doubling. Authority clips are stripped from the client prefab entirely. The temptation is to put that table on a wiki and ask designers to respect it. Make it a type, so the wrong thing is unrepresentable rather than merely discouraged.
