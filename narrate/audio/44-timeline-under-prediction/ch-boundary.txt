Say this one out loud, because everything else follows from it. Rollback restores component values. It does not restore entity existence. The prediction history backup is a memory copy of ghost components into a side buffer, and on restore it writes back only the fields that were serialised. There is no create and no destroy in it. An entity your clip spawned during a replayed tick is still there when that tick replays, and your clip will cheerfully spawn a second one. Netcode's answer is not to undo the spawn. It is to make the duplicate reconcilable.
