A root motion move driven entirely by a timeline really is a function of the identifier and the elapsed time. The character goes where the clip says regardless of what it brushes past, so both machines compute the same path from the same six bytes, and the transform does not need to replicate at all during the move. The moment a clip emits force into a dynamic body, that stops being true. The outcome now depends on contact, which is not derivable from the asset, so it has to go on the wire. Kinematic is compression. Force is a bandwidth commitment.
