Two Capsules
from the first clock edge to two capsules moving

A mechanism-first handbook for server-authoritative multiplayer. Written for someone who knows hardware and is new to this — every chapter bridges from something you already own: cache lines, DMA buffers, branch predictors, PLLs, dynamic linkers. The goal is not can you use this. The goal is could you write it.
